Known residual quirks of the japan model (covered by retained
`_OCR_CONFUSABLES` + NFKC normalization in the verifier): occasional
full-width digits (`２`), `引`→`弓` confusion (`取弓修飾`, `商品値弓！`),
date separator misread (`07722`). None affect the SC-001 term list.

code; the DEFAULT-ch pass in `ocr-japan-compare.txt` was produced through
exactly this path.
